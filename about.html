Boundless Alliance was created to give pilots the freedom to fly any airline, any aircraft, and any route without being restricted to a single virtual airline.

Why We Exist

Most virtual airlines limit pilots to a single airline. We wanted something different.

Boundless Alliance allows pilots to fly any real-world airline, any aircraft, and any route while still enjoying a structured community, economy, and PIREP system.
